INDEX.HTML · INDEX VIEWUsing the Call Index
01 Search every word spoken
Search covers every transcript, summary, cue, and
number; quote a phrase to match it in order. Smart
search ranks calls by match and also finds related
words (cop finds police), each passage tagged Exact
words, 2 of 3 words, or Related words. Exact words
only lists calls with every word typed.
02 Filter and sort
Narrow by date, number, outcome, length, relevance,
or speaker (the Defendant / Outside party chips
count one side's words); the High / Medium / Low
counts are clickable filters too. A search lists the best
match first; click a heading to sort another way.
03 Expand a row
Select a row for its summary, notes, and transcript.
Star calls, mark them reviewed, override relevance,
or name a number with exceptions for individual
calls. Changes apply to the index and charts;
transcript text and PDFs stay as delivered. Select
Original to reset relevance.
04 Open the source
Excerpts and transcript lines open the viewer at that
moment. Notes cite Tr. page:line and link to
the PDF. Select Pin note to collect a note in Pinned
notes; the index filters also apply there. Select
Pinned to remove it.

INDEX.HTML · CALENDAR AND TIMELINECalendar and Timeline
01 Switch views
The List, Calendar, and Timeline buttons under the
case name show the same calls three ways. The
search box and filters apply to all three, so a search
shows when a subject came up.
02 Read the calendar
Each day is shaded by how many calls it holds. A
crimson square marks a day with a High call, a
hollow square a Medium one. Point at a day for its
calls; click it to list them. A case longer than a year
shows one year at a time.
03 Read the timeline
Columns count calls per day, week, month, or year,
whichever fits the span of the case; the crimson part
of a column is its High calls. The rows beneath mark
where High and Medium calls fall. Click a column or a
mark to list those calls.
04 Where the dates come from
Call dates come from the facility's call records, or
from the recording file names when no records were
provided. A call without a date stays in the list and is
counted above the charts.

INDEX.HTML · CALL VIEWUsing the Call Viewer
01 Select a call
The left panel lists calls with their names, stars, and
reviewed marks. The header controls edit this call’s
review. Listening through essentially the whole
recording in one visit marks it reviewed
automatically; seeking to the end does not. A manual
unmark takes precedence.
02 Play and navigate the audio
SPACE plays and pauses; ← → skip five seconds;
the −15s / +15s buttons skip further. Red ticks on the
progress bar mark the call's notes.
03 Seek and search the transcript
Printed-format pages follow the audio with the
current line highlighted. Click any line or word to
jump to that exact moment; ↑ ↓ turn pages. The
search box shows every page containing a term;
“Return to playback” re-syncs.
04 Analysis panel and Present mode
The right panel shows the call's relevance, summary,
outside party, and clickable notes; both side panels
collapse with their ‹ › buttons. P enters Present
mode: audio controls and the transcript page only;
ESC exits.

CASE-REPORT.PDFUsing the Case Report
AT A GLANCE Delivery-level metrics: call volume over time, relevance distribution, and highlights such as the
most active day and most frequent caller.
TOP FINDINGS Cross-call findings synthesized from the highest-relevance calls, each citing its source call and
timestamp.
HIGH-RELEVANCE CALLS One card per HIGH call with its summary, outside-party identity, and timestamped notes quoting
the transcript.
MEDIUM-RELEVANCE CALLS Compact rows for MEDIUM calls: date, number, brief summary, and cue count.
CALLER STATISTICS Outside numbers ranked by volume, with call counts, total duration, active period, and the inferred
identity with its confidence level.
LINKED CITATIONS
Every citation in the report is a clickable link: call references open index.html at the cited moment, and PDF references open the
transcript. Keep case-report.pdf inside the delivery folder so its links can resolve.

SUMMARY SHEETS · INDEX.HTMLReading the Analysis
RELEVANCE RATINGS ASSIGNED PER CALL
HIGH Potentially jury-relevant or case-substantive content: charges, witnesses, evidence, admissions, or
statements bearing on the case.
MEDIUM Substantive legal or case context, not clearly central: court dates, counsel, custody status, or indirect
references to the case.
LOW Little to no apparent case relevance: routine personal conversation.
SUMMARY SECTIONS ON EACH TRANSCRIPT'S SUMMARY SHEET
NOTES Timestamped notes: only moments flagged as needing attorney attention. Each carries the audio
timestamp [MM:SS] and a TR. page:line cite that matches the printed transcript exactly.
“None flagged” means nothing on the call needed attention.
OUTSIDE PARTY Who the defendant appears to be speaking with, populated only when the call itself supports the
inference. Case-level identity inferences in the report carry a confidence level.
BRIEF SUMMARY A one- or two-sentence overview of the call.
