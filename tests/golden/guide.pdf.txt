INDEX.HTML · INDEX VIEWUsing the Call Index
01 Search every word spoken
Search covers every transcript, summary, cue,
and number; quote a phrase to match it in order.
Smart search ranks calls by match and also
finds passages that say it in other words, each
tagged Exact words, 2 of 3 words, or Similar
meaning. Exact words only lists calls with every
word typed.
02 Filter and sort
Narrow by date, number, outcome, length,
relevance, or speaker (the Defendant / Outside
party chips count one side's words); the High /
Medium / Low counts are clickable filters too. A
search lists the best match first; click a heading
to sort another way.
03 Expand a row
Selecting a row opens the call's summary,
outside-party identity, notes, and the full
transcript. The ‹ › arrows above the
transcript step through search matches, or
through the flagged passages when no search
is active.
04 Open the source
Excerpts, cues, and transcript lines open the
call view with the audio cued to that exact
moment. Each cue also cites the transcript PDF
by page and line (Tr. page:line), and the row
buttons open the call view or the transcript PDF
directly.

INDEX.HTML · CALENDAR AND TIMELINECalendar and Timeline
01 Switch views
The List, Calendar, and Timeline buttons under
the case name show the same calls three ways.
The search box and filters apply to all three, so
a search shows when a subject came up.
02 Read the calendar
Each day is shaded by how many calls it holds.
A crimson square marks a day with a High call,
a hollow square a Medium one. Point at a day
for its calls; click it to list them. A case longer
than a year shows one year at a time.
03 Read the timeline
Columns count calls per day, week, month, or
year, whichever fits the span of the case; the
crimson part of a column is its High calls. The
rows beneath mark where High and Medium
calls fall. Click a column or a mark to list those
calls.
04 Where the dates come from
Call dates come from the facility's call records,
or from the recording file names when no
records were provided. A call without a date
stays in the list and is counted above the charts.

INDEX.HTML · CALL VIEWUsing the Call Viewer
01 Select a call
The left panel lists every call with its date,
number, and relevance. Selecting a call loads
its audio and transcript. The Call Index link in
the top band, or the browser's back button,
returns to the index with its search and filters
intact.
02 Play and navigate the audio
SPACE plays and pauses; ← → skip five
seconds; the −15s / +15s buttons skip further.
Red ticks on the progress bar mark the call's
notes.
03 Seek and search the transcript
Printed-format pages follow the audio with the
current line highlighted. Click any line or word
to jump to that exact moment; ↑ ↓ turn
pages. The search box shows every page
containing a term; “Return to playback” re-
syncs.
04 Analysis panel and Present mode
The right panel shows the call's relevance,
summary, outside party, and clickable notes;
both side panels collapse with their ‹ ›
buttons. P enters Present mode: audio controls
and the transcript page only; ESC exits.

SUMMARY SHEETS · INDEX.HTMLReading the Analysis
RELEVANCE RATINGS ASSIGNED PER CALL
HIGH Potentially jury-relevant or case-substantive content: charges, witnesses, evidence,
admissions, or statements bearing on the case.
MEDIUM Substantive legal or case context, not clearly central: court dates, counsel, custody status,
or indirect references to the case.
LOW Little to no apparent case relevance: routine personal conversation.
SUMMARY SECTIONS ON EACH TRANSCRIPT'S SUMMARY SHEET
NOTES Timestamped notes: only moments flagged as needing attorney attention. Each carries
the audio timestamp [MM:SS] and a TR. page:line cite that matches the printed
transcript exactly. “None flagged” means nothing on the call needed attention.
OUTSIDE PARTY Who the defendant appears to be speaking with, populated only when the call itself
supports the inference. Case-level identity inferences in the report carry a confidence
level.
BRIEF SUMMARY A one- or two-sentence overview of the call.
