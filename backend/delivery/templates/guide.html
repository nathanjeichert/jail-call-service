01

Search every word spoken

Search covers every transcript, summary, cue, and number; quote a phrase to match it in order. Smart search ranks calls by match and also finds passages that say it in other words, each tagged Exact words, 2 of 3 words, or Similar meaning. Exact words only lists calls with every word typed.

02

Filter and sort

Narrow by date, number, outcome, length, relevance, or speaker (the Defendant / Outside party chips count one side's words); the High / Medium / Low counts are clickable filters too. A search lists the best match first; click a heading to sort another way.

03

Expand a row

Selecting a row opens the call's summary, outside-party identity, review cues, and the full transcript. The ‹ › arrows above the transcript step through search matches, or through the flagged passages when no search is active.

04

Open the source

Excerpts, cues, and transcript lines open the call view with the audio cued to that exact moment. Each cue also cites the transcript PDF by page and line (Tr. page:line), and the row buttons open the call view or the transcript PDF directly.

01

Switch views

The List, Calendar, and Timeline buttons under the case name show the same calls three ways. The search box and filters apply to all three, so a search shows when a subject came up.

02

Read the calendar

Each day is shaded by how many calls it holds. A crimson square marks a day with a High call, a hollow square a Medium one. Point at a day for its calls; click it to list them. A case longer than a year shows one year at a time.

03

Read the timeline

Columns count calls per day, week, month, or year, whichever fits the span of the case; the crimson part of a column is its High calls. The rows beneath mark where High and Medium calls fall. Click a column or a mark to list those calls.

04

Where the dates come from

Call dates come from the facility's call records, or from the recording file names when no records were provided. A call without a date stays in the list and is counted above the charts.

Call view screenshot

01

Select a call

The left panel lists every call with its date, number, and relevance. Selecting a call loads its audio and transcript. The Call Index link in the top band, or the browser's back button, returns to the index with its search and filters intact.

02

Play and navigate the audio

SPACE plays and pauses; ← → skip five seconds; the −15s / +15s buttons skip further. Red ticks on the progress bar mark the call's review cues.

03

Seek and search the transcript

Printed-format pages follow the audio with the current line highlighted. Click any line or word to jump to that exact moment; ↑ ↓ turn pages. The search box shows every page containing a term; “Return to playback” re-syncs.

04

Analysis panel and Present mode

The right panel shows the call's relevance, summary, outside party, and clickable review cues; both side panels collapse with their ‹ › buttons. P enters Present mode: audio controls and the transcript page only; ESC exits.

Using the Case ReportCASE-REPORT.PDF

At a Glance Delivery-level metrics: call volume over time, relevance distribution, and highlights such as the most active day and most frequent caller.
Top Findings Cross-call findings synthesized from the highest-relevance calls, each citing its source call and timestamp.
High-Relevance Calls One card per HIGH call with its summary, outside-party identity, and timestamped review cues quoting the transcript.
Medium-Relevance Calls Compact rows for MEDIUM calls: date, number, brief summary, and cue count.
Caller Statistics Outside numbers ranked by volume, with call counts, total duration, active period, and the inferred identity with its confidence level.

Linked Citations

Every citation in the report is a clickable link: call references open index.html at the cited moment, and PDF references open the transcript. Keep case-report.pdf inside the delivery folder so its links can resolve.

Reading the AnalysisSUMMARY SHEETS · INDEX.HTML

Relevance Ratings ASSIGNED PER CALL
High Potentially jury-relevant or case-substantive content: charges, witnesses, evidence, admissions, or statements bearing on the case.
Medium Substantive legal or case context, not clearly central: court dates, counsel, custody status, or indirect references to the case.
Low Little to no apparent case relevance: routine personal conversation.
Summary Sections ON EACH TRANSCRIPT'S SUMMARY SHEET
Notes Timestamped review cues: only moments flagged as needing attorney attention. Each carries the audio timestamp [MM:SS] and a TR. page:line cite that matches the printed transcript exactly. “No relevant information found” means nothing was flagged.
Outside Party Who the defendant appears to be speaking with, populated only when the call itself supports the inference. Case-level identity inferences in the report carry a confidence level.
Brief Summary A one- or two-sentence overview of the call.
